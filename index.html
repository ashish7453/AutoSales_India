Definitions, sources and what's left out
Dispatch vs registration
Most carmakers report wholesale dispatches to dealers. Skoda-VW, JSW MG, Honda and Nissan figures are VAHAN registrations, so they are not strictly comparable with the rest.
YoY and MoM
YoY compares October 2026 with October 2025; MoM compares it with September 2026.
Totals
The passenger vehicle total sums the 11 carmakers listed; the two-wheeler total sums the six largest makers. Neither is the official industry total, which SIAM publishes mid-month.
Left out
VECV (Eicher) reported growth of 34.9% but no unit figure here. Mahindra's tractor sales fell by double digits without a published unit figure in our sources. Retail (FADA) data is not included.
Sources
Autocar India, company stock-exchange filings as reported by Asianet, Investywise, CMV360 and BikeJunction, published 1–4 November 2026.
